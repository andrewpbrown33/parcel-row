Privacy

What we collect

If you email us or start a trial: your name, email, and billing details (handled by our payment processor — card numbers never touch our servers). If you use the product: which counties and features you open, so we can support you and improve the room.

What we don't do

We don't sell your information, run third-party ad trackers, or share your activity with anyone. The marketing site sets no analytics cookies during the founding period.

About the map data

The property information in Parcelrow comes from public records — state cadastral rolls, county GIS layers, and county filing systems — and reflects what those records state, including owner names of record. Corrections to the public record should be directed to the issuing county; tell us too and we'll refresh on the next weekly rebuild.
