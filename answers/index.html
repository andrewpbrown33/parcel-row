Answers

The questions land people actually ask.

Straight answers on how a county's public record works — what it can tell you about a parcel, and what it can't. No gate, no form. The method behind parcelrow, written out.

Public records · cited at the source · rebuilt every Monday
How do you find underbuilt parcels in a county? Rank every parcel by the gap between what it has built and what its zoning allows — and rebuild the ranking before it goes stale. Who owns a property held by an LLC? The path from an entity on the tax roll to the human behind it, and to every other parcel that owner controls. What is the difference between zoning and future land use? Zoning is what you can build today; future land use is the ceiling a rezoning can reach. The gap is where upzoning value sits. Where does county parcel data come from, and how current is it? Appraiser, clerk, statewide file, permits, corporate registry — and the refresh cadence that decides how stale your map is.
