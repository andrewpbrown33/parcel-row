The company behind the map room

Parcelrow is a Vantrow company.

The map room is one product, built for one trade: brokers, investors, and developers who work a county parcel by parcel. It came out of a studio that builds this way on purpose.

The family

Where the product came from

Vantrow builds software two ways. The studio embeds with a single firm and builds what it wishes it had. And when an engagement proves a whole category, the build becomes a company of its own — purpose-built for one industry, priced as a product, run on one standard: you approve everything, your data stays yours, and what you get is yours to keep. That's the row.

The studio

What Vantrow is

Vantrow is a software studio for operating companies — the firms that run the real economy on inboxes, spreadsheets, and software built for someone else. We embed with your team, learn how the firm actually works, and build what it wishes it had: a system of record shaped to your workflow, AI copilots that answer only from your own documents, automation that surfaces work before it slips. Every AI action is proposed, never committed — a human approves, everything is audited — and what we build is yours.
